### Stephen Drummond

I'm most interested in data engineering, machine learning, and the space where they meet: getting messy data into a shape a model or a dashboard can actually use.

**Projects**

- [**DaMuse**](https://github.com/StephenDrummond/DaMuse): Discord music bot with a group recommender. Tracks each listener's taste at the song, artist, and genre level from implicit signals (full listens, skips) and explicit likes, decays it over time, and picks the next track for whoever is in the channel. Python, PostgreSQL, Redis.
